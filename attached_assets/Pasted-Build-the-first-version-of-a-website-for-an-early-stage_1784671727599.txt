Build the first version of a website for an early-stage initiative called Vaeloryn.

About Vaeloryn

Vaeloryn is an early-stage initiative with the long-term mission of helping accelerate scientific, medical and technological progress, beginning in South Africa and ultimately contributing to humanity.

Core message:

Begin in South Africa. Build for humanity.

Vaeloryn aims to help create an ecosystem that can identify exceptional people and ideas, connect them with expertise, capital and opportunities, support research and innovation, help promising technologies progress toward real-world application, and eventually create a sustainable cycle where successful innovation helps enable future innovation.

Areas of interest include:

* Medical science and biotechnology
* Energy and energy technology
* Fundamental scientific research
* Artificial intelligence and computing
* Engineering and robotics
* Aerospace and space technology
* Advanced manufacturing and materials
* Water and agricultural technology
* Climate and environmental technology
* Other emerging and frontier technologies

Current Stage

Vaeloryn is at a very early stage.

The website must NOT imply that Vaeloryn currently has:

* A large team
* Existing investments
* Funded research projects
* University partnerships
* An investment fund
* A cryptocurrency or token
* Large amounts of capital
* Established advisers or partners

Do not invent statistics, team members, partnerships, testimonials, projects, achievements or funding.

Do not mention the identity of the founder and do not discuss founder anonymity. The website should simply speak in the voice of Vaeloryn using “we” and “our”.

Do not position Vaeloryn as a cryptocurrency project.

Primary Purpose of the Website

The first website has three objectives:

1. Clearly explain Vaeloryn’s mission and long-term vision.
2. Attract experienced people who may be willing to advise or help build Vaeloryn.
3. Allow researchers, scientists, engineers, inventors and entrepreneurs to express interest in connecting with Vaeloryn about an idea or project.

Design Direction

The website should feel:

* Serious
* Scientific
* Forward-looking
* Premium
* Trustworthy
* Ambitious
* Minimal
* Modern

It should NOT look like:

* A meme coin website
* A typical cryptocurrency landing page
* A gaming website
* A flashy startup template

Avoid excessive animations, neon crypto aesthetics, coin graphics and hype language.

Use sophisticated typography, generous spacing and subtle visual elements inspired by science, engineering, energy and exploration.

Create an original, minimal Vaeloryn wordmark as text for now. Do not spend time building a complex logo.

The website must work well on desktop, tablet and mobile.

Technical Requirements

Keep the project architecture simple and inexpensive to run.

Use a modern lightweight web stack appropriate for Replit.

Avoid unnecessary dependencies.

Do not add blockchain functionality.

Do not create a cryptocurrency.

Do not add payment processing.

Do not add user accounts or authentication.

Do not integrate paid APIs.

Do not create an unnecessarily complex backend.

For this initial development version, create the forms and form validation, but if a backend or external service is required to actually send/store submissions, keep the form handling as a clearly marked placeholder and do not introduce a paid service.

Make the code clean and easy to expand later.

Add basic SEO metadata for Vaeloryn, but do not make unsupported claims.

Prioritise a polished, credible front-end website that can be developed privately before public launch.

Before finishing, review the entire website and ensure that no invented achievements, partners, team members, projects, funding amounts or statistics have been added.
