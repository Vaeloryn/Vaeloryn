Explain that laws and regulations relating to crypto assets, digital assets, fundraising and financial services continue to evolve across jurisdictions.

Future regulatory requirements could:
- Restrict certain activities.
- Require licensing or registration.
- Require KYC/AML or other compliance systems.

- Change how VAELO may be distributed, marketed or used.

4. SMART-CONTRACT & CYBERSECURITY RISK

Explain that blockchain systems and smart contracts may contain:
- Software vulnerabilities.
- Coding errors.
- Exploitable logic.
- Infrastructure failures.
- Wallet or private-key compromise.
- Third-party dependency failures.
- Network-level risks.

Explain that loss, theft or compromise of private keys can result in irreversible loss of digital assets.

Explain:

Anti-concentration measures may have practical and technical limitations.

7. LIQUIDITY RISK

Explain that there is no guarantee that a liquid market for VAELO will exist.

- Economic analysis.
- Technical development.
- Legal or regulatory review.
- Security review.
- Real-world market conditions.

- Human error.
- Signer compromise.
- Governance disputes.
- Operational failures.
- Conflicts of interest.

10. THIRD-PARTY & BLOCKCHAIN INFRASTRUCTURE RISK

11. TAX RISK
