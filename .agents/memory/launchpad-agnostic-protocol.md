---
name: Launchpad-agnostic VAELO
description: Durable boundary between the canonical VAELO protocol and future sale or distribution venues.
---

The canonical VAELO token, Genesis Distribution, Founder Vesting, and Base
Mainnet deployment package must remain independent of every launchpad or sale
platform. Fjord Foundry is only a potential venue under evaluation; it is not a
confirmed partner or a protocol dependency.

**Why:** The token must remain deployable and reusable if the eventual
distribution uses another platform, a direct mechanism, a private/public
distribution, or no immediate sale.

**How to apply:** Keep sale quantities, prices, payment assets, eligibility,
claims, wallet limits, fees, refunds, collateral, and venue mechanics in
separate external planning or integration documents. Do not add venue-specific
logic, addresses, dependencies, or claims of acceptance/approval to the
canonical contracts or public status copy.
